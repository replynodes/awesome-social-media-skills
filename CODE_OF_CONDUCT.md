# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

This project exists to help people build useful AI agent skills for social
media work. Everyone participating — maintainers, contributors, and users
filing issues — is expected to help keep it a welcoming, productive space.

**Do:**

- Be respectful in issues, pull requests, and discussions.
- Give specific, actionable feedback on submissions.
- Assume good faith, especially with first-time contributors.
- Disclose any commercial or affiliate interest in what you're submitting.

**Don't:**

- Post harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language.
- Submit spam, undisclosed advertising, or affiliate-only links.
- Deliberately submit misleading claims about a skill's or tool's results.
- Use this repository to promote unrelated products or services.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions, and
any other official communication channel for this repository.

## Enforcement

Violations can be reported by opening a confidential report to the
maintainers at the contact listed in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), or by
emailing `conduct@replynodes.com` (placeholder — update once a dedicated
address exists). Maintainers may edit, close, or remove content that
violates this code, and may warn, temporarily restrict, or ban contributors
whose behavior is harmful to the community.
